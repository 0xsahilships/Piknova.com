## 👋 Why Piknova exists

Most image software is too complex, too expensive, too bloated, or built for designers instead of the people who actually post things every day.

Think about what you really need to do most days. Split one photo into a 3x3 grid for an Instagram puzzle feed. Get a passport photo under 20 KB because a form won't take anything bigger. Turn a pile of iPhone HEIC files into JPGs. Cut the background out of a product shot. Blur a face, a number plate, or an email address before a screenshot goes public.

None of that should need a design degree, a desktop app, a subscription, or an account. And none of it should mean sending your private photos to someone else's server.

That's the whole idea behind Piknova: **one place for every everyday image job, where each tool does one thing well, opens instantly, and stays out of your way.** No heavy software. No complicated interfaces. No unnecessary friction. Just focused tools that solve real problems.

Piknova is built and run independently by **Sahil**, a developer from India. It isn't venture-backed, and new tools ship roughly once a week.

> [!TIP]
> **Not to be confused with:** Piknova (piknova.com) is an independent product. It is not affiliated with Pixnova AI, Picnik, or any other similarly named app or website.

## 🔒 Privacy and your images

Privacy isn't a feature we bolted on. It's how the tools are built.

- 🖥️ **Your images stay on your device.** Every tool runs in your browser by default, so a photo is processed on your own machine and isn't uploaded.
- 🤖 **Even the AI is local.** The background remover's model runs in your browser, so your photos are never sent off to be processed.
- 👤 **No account needed.** No tool asks you to sign up. If you choose to create an account, Piknova stores your email address and nothing more about you.
- ▶️ **YouTube downloaders use public links.** You paste a public video or channel link. For channel pictures and banners, Piknova looks up where the image lives, and the image itself comes straight from YouTube's own servers.
- 📊 **Analytics stay basic.** We count which tools get opened and which actions finish, so we know what to build next. Session recordings hide text, form fields and images.
- 🍪 **Consent comes first** for visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Nothing that writes to your device runs before you agree, and a footer link reopens the choice.

Read the full [privacy policy](https://www.piknova.com/privacy) or the short [privacy summary](https://www.piknova.com/privacy.md).

## 💸 Pricing: how it stays free

**Every Piknova tool is free.** Every export size and every format, with no plan caps and nothing held back for a paid tier.

So how does it pay the bills?

- 🏷️ **One homepage sponsor slot.** That's the one thing Piknova sells. A sponsor gets one logo, one name, one short line and one link for 30 days. It buys placement, **never a tool's output**, and every request is reviewed by hand.
- 📰 **Ads on the site** help cover the rest.

Interested in sponsoring? See [piknova.com/pricing](https://www.piknova.com/pricing).

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>What is Piknova?</b></summary>
<br />

Piknova ([piknova.com](https://www.piknova.com)) is a free collection of 40+ online image tools. You can compress, resize, convert, crop, split, merge, annotate, blur, redact and remove backgrounds from images, all in your browser, with no account and no watermark.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is Piknova really free?</b></summary>
<br />

Yes. Every tool is free with no account, no watermark, no export limits, no resolution caps and no daily cap. The site is funded by a homepage sponsor slot and ads, and neither one ever touches what a tool gives you.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does Piknova upload my images?</b></summary>
<br />

No, not by default. The tools process images inside your browser, on your own device. That includes the AI background remover, whose model runs locally.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need to sign up?</b></summary>
<br />

No. No tool requires an account. Open a tool and start working.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which image formats does Piknova support?</b></summary>
<br />

JPG, PNG and WebP across the board, AVIF in the converters and bulk tools, and HEIC and HEIF photos from iPhones in the HEIC converters. The bulk resizer also takes GIF.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I compress an image to exactly 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB?</b></summary>
<br />

Open the [Image Compressor](https://www.piknova.com/tools/image-compressor), type your target size in KB, and download. For common targets there are ready-made pages like [Compress Image to 20KB](https://www.piknova.com/tools/compress-image-to-20kb) and [Compress Image to 50KB](https://www.piknova.com/tools/compress-image-to-50kb).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How does the background remover work without uploading my photo?</b></summary>
<br />

It uses an AI model that runs inside your browser. The model downloads once (about 40 MB) and your browser caches it, so later runs start faster. Your photo never leaves your device.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I process many images at once?</b></summary>
<br />

Yes. The bulk compressor, bulk resizer and image converters take up to 50 images at a time, and the bulk background remover takes up to 10. Results download as a single zip.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does Piknova work on my phone?</b></summary>
<br />

Yes. Piknova runs in any modern browser on desktop or mobile, with nothing to install.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is Piknova the same as Pixnova or Picnik?</b></summary>
<br />

No. Piknova (piknova.com) is an independent product built in India. It has no connection to Pixnova AI, Picnik or any other similarly named service.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Who makes Piknova?</b></summary>
<br />

Piknova is built and run independently by Sahil, a developer from India. It isn't venture-backed.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is Piknova open source? Can I self-host it?</b></summary>
<br />

No. Piknova is closed-source, proprietary software. This repository holds documentation only, and the source code is private. See the [license](#-license).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How often are new tools added?</b></summary>
<br />

Roughly once a week. The roadmap aims for 200+ tools. See [the road to 200+ tools](#%EF%B8%8F-the-road-to-200-tools).

</details>
